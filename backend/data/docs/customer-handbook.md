# Customer Handbook

**Version 4.2** · Last updated 3 March 2025

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Seats included | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49 / month | 3 | Small teams getting started |
| Growth | $199 / month | 15 | Mid-market teams with integrations |
| Scale | $749 / month | Unlimited | Enterprise, annual contract only |

Accounts created before 1 July 2024 keep two additional free seats on the
Starter plan. This was reinstated on 20 August 2024.

## Refund policy

We offer a **full refund within 30 days** of the first payment on any plan, no
questions asked. After 30 days:

- **Monthly plans** are not refunded for the current period, but cancelling
  stops all future charges immediately. Access continues to the end of the paid
  period.
- **Annual plans** are refunded pro rata for whole unused months, minus any
  discount that was granted for paying annually.
- **Overcharges caused by our billing errors** are always refunded in full,
  regardless of how long ago they occurred.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days and return to the original payment
method. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

## Support

| Plan | First response SLA | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 24 business hours | Email |
| Growth | 8 business hours | Email, chat |
| Scale | 2 business hours | Email, chat, dedicated Slack |

Billing questions receive an 8-hour first response on all plans, including
Starter. This was introduced after the Q3 2024 support backlog.

## Cancellation

Customers can cancel from Settings → Billing at any time. There is no
cancellation fee and no notice period on monthly plans. Annual plans may be
cancelled at any time but the refund follows the pro-rata rule above.

Data is retained for **90 days** after cancellation, during which reactivating
restores everything. After 90 days the workspace and all its data are deleted
permanently and cannot be recovered.

## Pricing changes

Following the Q3 2024 postmortem, we commit to a **minimum of 60 days' notice**
before any price increase. Announcements must state the new price, the notice
period, the grandfathering terms, and the number of affected seats — in the
announcement email itself, not in a linked footnote.

## Data and privacy

Customer data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) for EU customers and in the US
(Virginia) for everyone else. We do not train models on customer data. A signed
DPA is available on request for Growth and Scale plans.
